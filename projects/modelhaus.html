Fine-Tuned Model

ModelHaus

A question answering model fine tuned on a single authoritative source: JP 3-12, the joint publication for cyberspace operations. ModelHaus covers the whole path from raw doctrine PDF to a working question answering service, including the dataset builder, the fine tuning pipeline, and the web interface.

What it is

General purpose language models answer doctrine questions from whatever they absorbed in training, which is a problem when the answer has to match a specific publication. ModelHaus takes the opposite approach: fine tune a model on question and answer pairs drawn directly from the text of JP 3-12, so its answers stay grounded in that document. The resulting model, called HausGPT, is published on Hugging Face, and the repository contains everything needed to rebuild it.

How it works

  • Dataset construction. A builder script extracts the text of the JP 3-12 PDF, splits it into context chunks, and generates question and answer pairs for each chunk with Claude, writing them in an instruction, input, and output format ready for fine tuning. The committed dataset holds 186 pairs.
  • Fine tuning. A training notebook fine tunes a GPT style model implemented in PyTorch on those pairs, teaching it the publication’s terminology and the short, direct answer style the dataset uses.
  • Web interface. A Flask app loads the fine tuned checkpoint from Hugging Face and answers questions in the browser, with adjustable temperature, top-p, and maximum answer length. If generation is unavailable or a question closely matches the dataset, the app falls back to keyword retrieval over the same question and answer pairs, so an answer always comes from the publication’s content.

Example questions and answers

These pairs are taken directly from the project’s committed JP 3-12 dataset, the same data the model was fine tuned on and the same data the web app retrieves from.

Q: What is the primary purpose of cyberspace operations?

A: The primary purpose of cyberspace operations is to achieve objectives in or through cyberspace.

Q: What are the three interrelated layers of the cyberspace layer model used to assist in planning and execution of cyberspace operations?

A: The three interrelated layers are physical network, logical network, and cyber-persona.

Q: Why might a cyberspace operation option be preferable for engaging certain targets?

A: A cyberspace operation may be preferable because it may offer low probability of detection and/or no associated physical damage.
